The women behind Mama Africa

She began
on the beach.
They built
a business.

Come and be part of the next chapter

Mama Africa Mafia was created by Zuby and is run by local women. Their work is rooted in the island, its community and a determination to build something of their own.

After years in tourist hospitality, Zuby lost her job during the pandemic. With her family relying on her income, she began selling food and drinks on the beach.

That beginning grew into a local restaurant, with other women joining the work. When guests started asking about island excursions, Zuby sought funding, invested in a boat and opened a new chapter on the water.

Today, Mama Africa brings together a local network of women and other people across the community. The ambition is simple: build an independent business, create opportunity and welcome visitors to Mafia Island.
