Snorkeling Trip Chole Bay

Explore the reefs of Chole Bay.

Explore this tour

Discover the underwater beauty of Chole Bay on a guided snorkeling adventure through some of Mafia Island's most fascinating coral reefs. Explore three different snorkeling locations, each offering unique coral formations, colorful tropical fish, and an incredible variety of marine life. The excursion is carefully planned around the tides to provide favorable visibility and snorkeling conditions. Between stops, relax aboard the boat and enjoy fresh fruit, drinking water, and light snacks while taking in the beautiful surroundings of Chole Bay.

A suggested day on the water

  1. Meeting at Swahili Local Restaurant

    Meet your guides, receive your snorkeling equipment, and make sure everything fits comfortably. Guests are welcome to use their own equipment.

  2. Boarding and Introduction

    Head to the beach, board the boat, and meet your captain and local snorkeling guides.

  3. First Snorkeling Stop

    Begin exploring the underwater world at one of Chole Bay's snorkeling locations, such as Big Rock, Small Rock, or Coral Garden, depending on sea conditions.

  4. Second Snorkeling Stop

    Travel by boat to another coral reef and discover different marine habitats and tropical fish.

  5. Third Snorkeling Stop

    Enjoy your final snorkeling session at a third location, with more opportunities to observe the diverse underwater life.

  6. Return to the Beach

    Relax during the boat journey back to your starting point.

Confirm snorkeling equipment, sea conditions and any park fees.

Chole Village Tour

Village paths, dhow builders and flying foxes.

Explore this tour

Step into the authentic island life of Chole, a peaceful tropical village located just off the coast of Mafia Island. Explore its narrow pathways, meet local residents, and discover the traditions that have shaped this small island community for generations. Observe the remarkable flying fox fruit bats, explore historical ruins, and learn about local activities such as seaweed farming and traditional wooden boat building. This experience offers a unique opportunity to connect with the people of Chole Island and discover a side of Mafia that goes far beyond its beautiful beaches.

A suggested day on the water

  1. Departure from Utende Beach

    Board a traditional boat for a scenic journey across the water to Chole Island.

  2. Village Walking Tour

    Explore the peaceful village on foot with a local guide and discover the daily life and traditions of the island community.

  3. Flying Fox Observation

    Visit areas inhabited by the island's impressive fruit bats and observe these fascinating animals in their natural environment.

  4. Historical Ruins

    Discover remnants of Chole's history and learn about the island's cultural heritage.

  5. Seaweed Farming Traditions

    Learn how local women collect, dry, and process seaweed for products such as soap and shampoo. Observe these activities when they are taking place.

  6. Traditional Dhow Boat Building

    Visit local boat-building areas and discover how skilled craftsmen construct traditional wooden dhow boats.

  7. Return to Utende Beach

    Board the boat for the return journey to Mafia Island.

Craft activity and sightings vary. Ask before photographing people.

Blue Lagoons and Kua Ruins

A lagoon and a walk through coastal history.

Explore this tour

Experience a fascinating combination of history, nature, and adventure on an excursion to the ancient Kua Ruins and the spectacular Blue Lagoon on Juani Island. Begin by exploring the remains of a historic Swahili settlement, where ancient stone structures and mosques tell the story of the island's rich cultural past. Continue by boat through peaceful mangrove channels until you reach the breathtaking Blue Lagoon, a secluded natural pool surrounded by lush tropical vegetation. Swim in its calm waters, admire the unique landscape, and observe the fascinating upside-down jellyfish that inhabit the lagoon. A memorable journey through some of the most remarkable natural and historical locations in the Mafia archipelago.

A suggested day on the water

  1. Departure from Utende Beach

    Board the boat and begin your journey toward Juani Island.

  2. Exploring Kua Ruins

    Walk among the historic stone ruins, discover ancient mosque remains, and learn about the settlement's cultural and trading history.

  3. Mangrove Channel Boat Ride

    Continue through a beautiful natural channel surrounded by mangrove forests, keeping an eye out for local wildlife.

  4. Arrival at Blue Lagoon

    Discover the stunning natural lagoon hidden among the mangroves.

  5. Swimming and Lagoon Exploration

    Enjoy time in the calm water and observe the lagoon's distinctive upside-down jellyfish without touching or disturbing them.

  6. Return Boat Journey

    Travel back through the mangrove channels and return to Utende Beach.

Confirm route, tide, access, boat arrangements and any park or entry fees.

Whale Shark Snorkeling

Search for whale sharks with a local crew.

Explore this tour

Experience one of Mafia Island's most extraordinary marine adventures: the opportunity to swim alongside magnificent whale sharks in their natural habitat. Begin your journey with a transfer to the departure point, where your local guides and boat captain will take you out into the ocean in search of these gentle giants. When a whale shark is spotted and conditions allow, enter the water with your snorkeling equipment for the unforgettable experience of observing the world's largest fish swimming gracefully through the sea. The women behind Mama Africa

She began
on the beach.
They built
a business.

Come and be part of the next chapter

Mama Africa Mafia was created by Zuby and is run by local women. Their work is rooted in the island, its community and a determination to build something of their own.

After years in tourist hospitality, Zuby lost her job during the pandemic. With her family relying on her income, she began selling food and drinks on the beach.

That beginning grew into a local restaurant, with other women joining the work. When guests started asking about island excursions, Zuby sought funding, invested in a boat and opened a new chapter on the water.

Today, Mama Africa brings together a local network of women and other people across the community. The ambition is simple: build an independent business, create opportunity and welcome visitors to Mafia Island.
